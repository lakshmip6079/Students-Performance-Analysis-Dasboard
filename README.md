Problem Statement:
Educational institutions need to monitor students  academic performance to identify students who are struggling in their studies.
Currently, teachers may find it difficult to identify students who are performing poorly in individual subjects, students who are at risk of failing, and students who need additional academic support to improve their grades.
The objective of this project is to develop an interactive Power BI dashboard that analyses student marks, grades and academic performance.
