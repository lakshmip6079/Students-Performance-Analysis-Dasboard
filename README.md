Problem Statement:
Educational institutions need to monitor students  academic performance to identify students who are struggling in their studies.
Currently, teachers may find it difficult to identify students who are performing poorly in individual subjects, students who are at risk of failing, and students who need additional academic support to improve their grades.
The objective of this project is to develop an interactive Power BI dashboard that analyses student marks, grades and academic performance.<img width="929" height="522" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b33fb345-e8c0-4f13-8cba-a22d3d999213" />

Key Insights:
Insight 1:
A large percentage of students contribute to Failing grades.
Insight 2:
Most students are concentrated in High academic support.
Insight 3:
Fail count is more Maths subject
Insight 4:
More students need academic support, indicating limited academic excellence.
Insight 5:
Pass percentage provides an overall indicator of school academic performance.

Story Telling :
"Our dashboard analyses the academic performance of students across different subjects and grades.
We began by analysing the total student population and identified the overall pass and fail percentages.
Subject-wise analysis revealed which subjects have stronger and weaker performance. This helps teachers focus on areas where students struggle the most.
The Academic support chart shows the academic spread of students. We identified students in  Critical support  as the primary group requiring additional academic support.
Using the Top 10 Students chart, we identified the highest-performing students and academic achievers.
Overall, the dashboard provides a comprehensive view of student performance and helps school management make informed decisions regarding academic improvement initiatives."
